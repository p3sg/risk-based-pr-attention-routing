# Three-part YouTube series

This is P3's application standing in for an adopter's backend, frontend or both.
Viewers adopt the router and workflow, **not** the dashboard or its Google login.

1. **Route attention.** Explain deterministic rules, contextual rubric and durable
   docs. Run package tests and inspect a floor/AI final result. A hypothetical
   isolated wording change is LOW; it still requires a human reviewer.
2. **Publish trustworthy evidence.** Walk the verification-triggered workflow,
   trusted policy vs untrusted diff, deterministic fallback, structured record and
   change-only notifications. Propose changing a shared queue contract (MEDIUM)
   and weakening identity checks (HIGH). Do not actually weaken authentication.
3. **Observe a real queue.** Sign in privately, open an approved real repository,
   select a PR and show its current/stale/unverified evidence and workflow link.
   Refresh: no classification is triggered. Explain why no DB or model key exists
   in the reader and why an npm consumer need not deploy this application.

These are illustrative change scenarios, not fake PR data or performance metrics.
Use actual PRs/results during recording. If an artifact has expired or a dimension
was not recorded, show that limitation rather than manufacturing evidence.
Same-commit AI dedup/reliable delivery retries are follow-up demonstrations; do not
claim they are implemented by this reader. Obtain permission before exposing a
private repository, redact sensitive content, and never record secret configuration.
